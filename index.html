About

Working at the intersection of wet lab biology and computational analysis, I build robust scripts and tools that bridge the bench and the terminal. My work thus far spans primer design, assay validation, restriction enzyme mapping, DNA fragment prediction, and quality-control pipelines for next-generation sequencing libraries.


I believe the natural world is not uniform. It is dynamic and chaotic. Therefore, I write code that embraces that reality rather than hiding behind oversimplified assumptions and hopeful maybes. I ride the chaos.

Projects

R_Assay_Validation_Report

An R Markdown script that converts raw multiplex qPCR/ddPCR-derived fluorescence data into a human-readable pass/fail validation report. Metrics include flagging off-target signals, low abundance, and failed primer 4-plex interacrtions. The intention is to enable a lab RA to can act fast with new assays that perform out-of-the-box, or to rapidly escalate the sepcific assay issue to senior staff.

R_DNA_digest_fragments

Restriction Enzyme DNA Digest Prediction (RE-D-DiP) is an R script that uses BiocManager and DECIPHER to analyze real genomic sequences and plot actual fragment-size distributions. The purpose is to reveal that RE cut sites are anything but uniformly distributed across the human genome, as well as other genomes, and therefore assist in picking enzymes or combination of REs to tune enzymatic digestion to the size needed for downstream library prep and NGS.

Py_motif_search_counter

A Python tool for searching DNA for restriction endonuclease binding motifs, including support for N-length motifs and full IUPAC degeneracy, searched across a multi-entry FASTA file. The original intent was designed to locate methylation-sensitive RE sites within cancer gene promoters. Special methylation-dependent REs could then be applied to a population of molecules to test for hypo/hyper methylation given these identified motifs. The script output, per-entry in the FASTA file, is a CSV file of motif positions and cut offsets.

Py_16S_chimera_hunter

A two-part Python pipeline for quality-controlling 16S long-read sequencing data. Part one generates mock FASTA contigs with controllable chimeric, truncated, and undefined fractions. Part two hunts chimeric ends by anchoring V1/V9 regions against a Zymo microbial standard. The output is a text file summary of the match metrics and count/percentage bar plots.
